Meta Ads

Meta Ads Basics

A beginner's guide to planning your first Meta ad campaign.

By Saiful Islam Mollah, Digital Marketing Specialist · October 12, 2026

What Meta ads are

Meta ads let a business show paid messages across Meta platforms such as Facebook and Instagram. You choose an objective, define who should see the ad, set a budget and provide creative. Because platform options change, check Meta's official Business Help Centre for current features.

Start with one clear goal

Pick one primary objective before you build anything: for example enquiries, website visits or messages. A clear goal decides the campaign objective, the creative and how you judge results. Example: a jewellery brand wanting enquiries should send people to a message or enquiry form, not just chase reach.

Know your audience

Describe who you want to reach: location, interests, and what problem your product solves. Start with a focused audience, then review results before widening it.

Creative and message

Use a clear image or short video, one main message and one call to action. Test two or three variations rather than changing everything at once.

Read the results

Review results against your goal on a regular schedule. Look at cost per result, click-through rate and the quality of enquiries, not only reach.

Checklist

  • Write down one goal
  • Describe your audience in a sentence
  • Prepare 2–3 creative variations
  • Set a budget you are comfortable with
  • Set a review date
