Privacy

How this website handles information.

What we collect

This website does not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies.

Enquiry form

When you send the enquiry form on the Contact page, the details you type (name, business, email, optional phone, service, timeline, budget and project brief) are saved in this website’s database so the owner can read and reply to them. They are visible only to the owner after login, are not shared or sold, and are deleted on request. The website does not send email on your behalf. You can also write by email, call, or use WhatsApp directly; those services handle those messages under their own policies.

Owner login

The site owner signs in to an admin area using a secure session cookie. No visitor accounts exist.

Hosting

Hosting providers may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times.
