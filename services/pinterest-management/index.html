Pinterest Management for Visual Discovery

Pinterest works like a visual search engine. We set up boards and pins, write search-friendly titles and descriptions and schedule pins that point people to your site or shop.

Enquire about Pinterest Management →WhatsApp

Who it is for

Brands in fashion, home, food, events and other visual categories with content or products to show.

Problems it addresses

  • Boards without a clear theme
  • Pins with weak titles and no link
  • No routine for refreshing pins

Scope

What you get

  1. Profile and board structure
  2. Pin titles, descriptions and keyword use
  3. Pin scheduling
  4. Pin design briefs (design production is separate)
  5. Analytics review

Workflow

How it works with you

You stay involved at each stage and approve the work before it goes live.

  1. Understand

    We learn about your business, audience and goals first.

  2. Plan

    We agree priorities, scope and a calendar with you.

  3. Create & Launch

    We produce the agreed work and publish it with your approval.

  4. Review & Improve

    We report what happened and use it to improve the next cycle.

What we need from you

  • Account access
  • Product images or content assets
  • Website links for pins

Reporting and review

A monthly review covers impressions, saves and outbound clicks, with pin ideas for the next month.
