TikTok Ads Management

TikTok Ads puts short video in front of chosen audiences. We plan the campaign, prepare creative briefs, set up ads in TikTok Ads Manager and refine them through testing.

Enquire about TikTok Ads →WhatsApp

Who it is for

Brands with a product or offer that suits short vertical video and a budget to test.

Problems it addresses

  • Ads that look like ads and get skipped
  • Unclear audiences and objectives
  • No testing of hooks or formats

Scope

What you get

  1. Objective and audience planning
  2. Creative briefs for short video ads
  3. Campaign setup in TikTok Ads Manager
  4. Testing of hooks, formats and audiences
  5. Optimisation and reporting

Workflow

How it works with you

You stay involved at each stage and approve the work before it goes live.

  1. Understand

    We learn about your business, audience and goals first.

  2. Plan

    We agree priorities, scope and a calendar with you.

  3. Create & Launch

    We produce the agreed work and publish it with your approval.

  4. Review & Improve

    We report what happened and use it to improve the next cycle.

What we need from you

  • Ad account access
  • Video creative or a production plan
  • A product page or landing page
  • A budget you set

Reporting and review

Reports cover spend, video engagement and results tied to your goal, with test outcomes and next steps.
